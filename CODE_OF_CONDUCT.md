# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want FileX to be a welcoming project for everyone — whatever your experience, background,
language or identity. Contributions in Bangla and English are equally welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and explain decisions when you are one.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when you represent
the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/dev-sajid007). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. Responses are proportionate:
usually a private warning first, escalating only for repeated or serious behaviour.
